OFFICIAL APPLICATION ROUTE

Ready for your next course?

All MUHAS short-course applications are submitted through the official REDCap application system.

Open application form ↗
  1. Find your course. Note its title and code in the catalogue.
  2. Confirm the intake. A catalogue listing does not necessarily mean applications are currently open.
  3. Submit your application. Select the corresponding course in the official form. Applicants are notified by email through REDCap.
