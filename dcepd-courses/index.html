School of Biomedical Sciences

Participants will learn how to design, prototype, manufacture, and evaluate replacement parts for biomedical equipment, assistive devices, and clinical training models using computer-aided design (CAD) and 3D printing. BIOMEDICAL AND PHARMACEUTICAL SCIENCES BRIDGING COURSE FOR PHARMACY GRADUATES

School of Pharmacy

This is a targeted multidisciplinary CPD bridging course for pharmacy graduates who obtained their BPharm degree through a lateral-entry route after prior diploma-level pharmacy training, particularly the three-year lateral-entry BPharm pathway used by the target graduates in India. In the lateral-entry system, prior diploma learning is recognized and the student joins the degree programme at an advanced level rather than repeating all foundational content. When the training background of these graduates was considered in relation to the four-year BPharm programme used in Tanzania, the Pharmacy Council highlighted specific gaps in selected basic biomedical and pharmaceutical sciences.
